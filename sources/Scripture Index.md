# Scripture Index

Every passage the vault cites, with the original-language notes that matter to the argument. Verify wording against a critical text before anything goes public.

## The tearing / fabric motif
| Passage | Role | Language note |
|---|---|---|
| Isaiah 40:22 | Heavens stretched "like a curtain… a tent" | One of many *nāṭâ* ("stretch out") texts |
| Isaiah 42:5; 44:24; Job 9:8; Psalm 104:2; Jeremiah 10:12; Zechariah 12:1 | The stretched-heavens formula repeated | Same verb family — an established image, not a one-off |
| Isaiah 64:1 | "Oh, that You would rend the heavens and come down" | Hebrew *qāraʿ* — to tear (used of tearing garments); = Isaiah 63:19b in Hebrew (MT) versification |
| Mark 1:10 | Heavens "torn open" at the baptism | Greek *schizō*; Matthew 3:16 / Luke 3:21 use *anoigō* ("opened") instead |
| Mark 15:38 | Temple veil "torn in two, top to bottom" | Same verb, *schizō* — Mark's only two uses (concordance-verified); inclusio (Ulansey, *JBL* 110/1, 1991, pp. 123–125). Josephus, *War* 5.212–214 (Whiston): "a kind of image of the universe… all that was mystical in the heavens, excepting that of the [twelve] signs" — "panorama of the heavens" is Thackeray's Loeb rendering |
| Revelation 6:14; Isaiah 34:4 | Sky recedes/rolls up "like a scroll" | Heavens as a coherent removable sheet |
| Genesis 1:6–8; Job 37:18 | The *rāqîaʿ* (firmament), "hard as a cast-metal mirror" | Basis for the solid-dome reading (C24) |

## Orion / stars
| Passage | Role | Language note |
|---|---|---|
| Job 9:9; Job 38:31; Amos 5:8 | Orion (*kesîl*) and Pleiades (*kîmâ*) named; "can you loosen the cords (*mōšekhôt*, not 'belt') of Orion?" | *Kesîl* literally "fool"; word study done ([[C15 Kesil Word Study]]): traditionally and probably Orion — versions' "giant" = Semitic name of Orion (fettered-Nimrod tradition) — but witnesses late and inconsistent (LXX: Hesperus at Job 9:9, dropped at Amos 5:8); `#claim/contested` leaning solid |
| Psalm 19:1 | "The heavens declare the glory of God" | The charter text for Version C |
| Isaiah 45:18 | Earth "formed to be inhabited" | Used in the Designed Address argument (C14) |
| Jude 13 | "Wandering stars" | *Planētes asteres* = the planets in standard ancient usage — see C8, retired as a black-hole reference |
| Exodus 20:21; 1 Kings 8:12 | God dwelling in "thick darkness" | Lead for the O8 candidate answer |
