# The Hypothesis

**Raw form:** the Milky Way — the glowing band across the night sky — is a tear in the fabric of the heavens through which the light of Heaven leaks into the physical world.

That sentence actually contains three different claims, and they stand or fall separately. Everything in the stress-test layer refers to these by letter.

## Version A — the physical claim
*The band's light is literally light from another realm passing through a physical rupture in spacetime.*

This is an empirical claim about photons, and it is testable: if any of the band's light came from outside known astrophysics, it would show up as an unexplained emission component. Status after the audit: **falsified** — see [[Objections Ledger]] O1–O3. Every photon in the band is accounted for by resolved stars, gas, and dust with normal spectra, parallaxes, and proper motions.

## Version B — the metaphysical claim
*Heaven is a higher-dimensional realm co-located with our space; the galactic disk (or its center) marks a region of overlap where the barrier between realms is thin — not detectable as anomalous light, but real.*

This is coherent and cannot be ruled out — but by construction it predicts nothing observable, so it also cannot be supported by any astronomical evidence. It lives or dies on theological and philosophical grounds, not scientific ones. Status: **unfalsifiable — permitted as metaphysics, banned as science**. See O6, O8.

## Version C — the typological claim
*The Milky Way is a designed sign: a real, physical structure whose form and our placement within it function the way scripture says the heavens function — declaring, prefiguring, teaching. The "tear" is not in the sky but in the pattern: God marks divine entry by tearing (Mark's schizō, the temple veil), and the band across the sky is the visible emblem of that pattern.*

This makes no contested physical claims at all. It inherits the strongest material from the foundation (the schizō inclusio in Mark, the Josephus temple-veil cosmology, the path-of-souls folklore, the habitability of our galactic address) without needing the raqia to be solid or the light to be leaking. Status: **defensible — this is where the steelman lives**. See [[Steelman]].

## Standing decision
Per [[Rules of Evidence]]: A is retired as a physical claim (kept as the historical origin of the idea), B is held as explicit metaphysics, and C is the version being actively built and stressed.
