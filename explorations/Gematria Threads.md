## Thread 1 — Firmament = Thick Darkness (380) `#claim/pattern`
**רקיע (raqia) = ערפל (araphel) = 380.** The vault's two structural terms carry the same value: the *firmament* — the solid sky of the mainstream raqia reading (no ancient text puts the Milky Way in it as a seam; that image is our modern reconstruction — [[06 Heaven Leaking Through]], C31) — and the *thick darkness* in which God dwells (Exod 20:21; 1 Kings 8:12; [[O8 Divine Darkness]]). Read as a pattern: the barrier and the veil are numerically one — the same 380 whether heaven's boundary is pictured as hammered brightness (Job 37:18) or concealing darkness. Our observation, provenance open (no classical source found yet).
**Arithmetic shown (protocol back-fill, review round 1):** רקיע = ר200 + ק100 + י10 + ע70 = **380**; עֲרָפֶל = ע70 + ר200 + פ80 + ל30 = **380**. Spellings are the MT consonantal forms (רָקִיעַ Gen 1:6; עֲרָפֶל Exod 20:21), bare nouns without prefixes or articles (our standing policy: prefixes/articles excluded).

## Thread 2 — Ladder = Sinai (130) `#claim/pattern`, classical
**סלם (sulam) = סיני (Sinai) = 130** — a classical correspondence, and older than we first recorded: **it is stated in Genesis Rabbah 68:12 itself** ("the letters of this equal the letters of that"; the angels are Moses and Aaron), with the Baal HaTurim (d. c. 1340, on Gen 28:12) repeating it and adding plene-spelling values (סולם = 136 = *mamon*; his *kol* value is source-inconsistent and unusable pending a spelling check) — attribution corrected in round 2, see [[Jacobs Ladder Reception]]. Jacob's ladder and Sinai are the Torah's two places where earth and heaven are formally *joined*, and they share a value. This lands directly on the steelman's path-between-worlds thread — because Jacob's ladder scene ends: "How awesome is this place! This is none other than the house of God, and **this is the gate of heaven**" (Gen 28:17) — terror (*mysterium tremendum*, [[O8 Divine Darkness]]) at a place where the barrier opens, with traffic (angels ascending and descending) on the open path.
