# Objections Ledger

The strongest objections we can construct, at full strength, per [[Rules of Evidence]]. Each names which version of [[The Hypothesis]] it hits (A = physical tear, B = dimensional overlap, C = typological sign), a severity, and a status. An objection is only **retired** when its written answer actually answers it.

---

## O1 — The photon budget is closed
**Hits: A. Severity: fatal. Status: upheld — forced A's retirement.**
If any of the band's light leaked from another realm, it would appear as an unexplained emission component. It doesn't. The band's brightness, spectrum, and structure are fully reproduced by star counts, stellar population models, gas, and dust. Gaia has individually cataloged ~1.8 billion of the stars that make it up, each with normal spectra, parallax, and proper motion. There is no remainder for Heaven to be.
*Answer on file:* none possible for A. Version A is retired ([[The Hypothesis]]); C is untouched because C never claimed the light was anomalous.

## O2 — The band is geometry, not a seam
**Hits: A. Severity: fatal (independent of O1). Status: upheld.**
The band is a **great circle** through the sky — visible in both hemispheres, wrapping 360°, including toward the galactic *anticenter* where there is no "center glow" at all. That is exactly what "observer embedded in a disk" predicts, and not at all what "a localized rip in a dome" predicts. Any rip hypothesis has to explain why the tear happens to pass precisely through every observer's celestial sphere as a great circle — the disk explanation gets this for free.
*Answer on file:* none for A. Notably, this objection *helps* C: the form of the band is a necessary consequence of our address (Section 1), which is the part of the foundation that survives strongest.

## O3 — Resolved since 1610
**Hits: A. Severity: fatal (third independent line). Status: upheld.**
Galileo pointed a telescope at the band and it dissolved into individual stars (*Sidereus Nuncius*, 1610). A seam of divine light should not resolve into billions of ordinary point sources under magnification. Four centuries of increasing resolution have only deepened this.
*Answer on file:* none for A.

## O4 — The genetic fallacy cut
**Hits: A, B. Severity: severe. Status: upheld — reshaped the whole project.**
The historical argument in [[06 Heaven Leaking Through]] shows why ancients *would* read the band as a crack in the raqia. But explaining why a belief arose is not evidence the belief is true — especially when its load-bearing premise (a solid dome) is known to be false. The foundation's most persuasive section is support for the *history of an idea*, not for the idea.
*Answer on file (partial):* accepted in full for A/B. For C the history is legitimately load-bearing in a different way: C claims the band *functions as a sign*, and the cross-cultural record (C25) is direct evidence that it has in fact functioned that way for millennia. Signs are constituted by how they're read.

## O5 — Anachronism / retrofitting
**Hits: A, B, and sloppy versions of C. Severity: moderate. Status: upheld, enforced by tags.**
Mapping *abyssos*, Jude's wandering stars, and tzimtzum onto GR objects reads modern physics back into texts whose referents were their own cosmology. Concordism has a bad track record: every generation finds its own science "uncannily" in the same verses.
*Answer on file:* accepted. Enforced by [[Rules of Evidence]] — everything of this shape is tagged `interpretation`/`misreading` and barred from load-bearing use (C8 retired outright). C survives because typology claims patterns, not predictions.

## O6 — Unfalsifiability of the overlap claim
**Hits: B. Severity: severe (as science); null (as metaphysics). Status: upheld with a boundary.**
Version B predicts nothing observable, so no observation can support it either; as science it's not wrong, it's not even in the game. Occam removes it.
*Answer on file:* B is re-classed as explicit metaphysics and banned from borrowing scientific credibility. It may still be *believed*; it may not be *evidenced* by astronomy. That boundary is now written into [[The Hypothesis]].

## O7 — Selection effect vs. design
**Hits: the "Designed Address" support (Section 2). Severity: moderate. Status: open — best remaining live argument.**
Observers only ever find themselves somewhere habitable, so "our address is favorable" is guaranteed by survivorship and carries no design information by itself. The design reading needs the stronger fine-tuning form of the argument, which is a real, live philosophical debate (anthropic reasoning vs. design inference) — not settled either way.
*Answer on file (partial):* Section 2 demoted from "proof" to "consonance": a providential reading consistent with the facts, argued properly only inside the wider fine-tuning debate. Kept open on purpose.

## O8 — The center is the least heavenly place
**Hits: B (and rhetoric in Section 6). Severity: moderate. Status: open.**
The literal galactic center is a dust-shrouded region around Sgr A*, a 4-million-solar-mass black hole ringed by sterilizing radiation — an odd pick for the point where Heaven bleeds in. And the visible "glow" toward the center isn't the center at all (C12).
*Answer on file (candidate, not yet accepted):* a theological reversal is available — scripture repeatedly locates God's self-revelation in darkness and dread (Exodus 20:21, "thick darkness"; 1 Kings 8:12), so "the barrier's thin place is terrifying" is not obviously disqualifying. Logged as a lead, not an answer.

## O9 — The tear reading isn't universal
**Hits: the "entirely logical" claim in Section 6. Severity: minor. Status: upheld.**
Cultures read the band as a river (China), a road (Rome, Norse), spilled milk (Greece), a bird path (Baltic), and a dark constellation's silhouette (Andean; Australian Great Emu). If the band self-evidently read as a tear in the dome, the readings would converge. They don't.
*Answer on file:* accepted; Section 6's wording weakened from "entirely logical" to "very natural for a dome cosmology." The diversity actually feeds C's folklore evidence base (C25) — nearly all the readings agree it is a *path between worlds*, which is the part C uses.
