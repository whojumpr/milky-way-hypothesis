# Claims Audit

Every factual claim in the foundation, numbered, checked, and tagged per [[Rules of Evidence]]. A claim's verdict here is what its foundation note must reflect. Sources are named so any verdict can be re-checked.

## Astronomy (Sections 1, 2, 5, 6)

| # | Claim | Verdict | Notes |
|---|-------|---------|-------|
| C1 | Milky Way is a disk galaxy; we see it edge-on from inside as a band | `#claim/solid` | Basic galactic astronomy; the band is the disk plane seen from within. |
| C2 | We live in the Orion Arm/Spur, a minor arm between the Sagittarius and Perseus arms | `#claim/solid` | Standard structure maps (masers/VLBI, Reid et al.); "spur" vs "arm" status is debated in detail but the placement is right. |
| C3 | ~26,000 ly from the galactic center | `#claim/solid` | GRAVITY collaboration (2019): ~26,700 ± ~1,000 ly. "Roughly 26,000" is fine. |
| C4 | Nearly all naked-eye stars are neighbors in/near our arm | `#claim/solid` | Most are within ~1,000–4,000 ly. Caveats: Deneb (~2,600 ly), and M31/the Magellanic Clouds are naked-eye and extragalactic. |
| C5 | Major arms are deadly; life is *only possible* because we're in a quiet minor arm | `#claim/contested` | Elevated supernova/GMC hazard in arms is real. But (a) the Sun's orbit carries it through arm regions over ~230-Myr revolutions; (b) the Galactic Habitable Zone literature (Lineweaver et al. 2004) is itself contested; (c) "only possible" is an overclaim no survey supports. Weakened form survives: our location is *favorable*. |
| C6 | A major-arm location would block our view of deep space; our spot enables discovery | `#claim/contested` | Overstated. Even here, the disk plane blocks ~10–20% of the extragalactic sky (zone of avoidance). Perpendicular-to-disk views are clear from most inter-arm positions, not uniquely ours. Weakened form survives (Privileged Planet argument, itself contested). |
| C7 | Black holes are literally bottomless pits in spacetime geometry | `#claim/interpretation` | Fair as metaphor. Physics caveat: an infalling object reaches the singularity in finite proper time — "bottomless" describes the geometry's lack of a floor, not endless falling. |
| C8 | Jude 13's "wandering stars" matches black holes (collapsed stars trapping light) | `#claim/misreading` | *Planētes asteres* = the planets, the standard ancient usage; the image is lights straying from course. Anachronistic as a black-hole reference. **Retired as support; keep only as poetry.** |
| C9 | Information falling into a black hole is permanently recorded on the event horizon | `#claim/contested` | The holographic principle is a major conjecture (’t Hooft, Susskind; AdS/CFT), not established fact for our universe; information-paradox resolution still debated. |
| C10 | The Orion Nebula is a massive stellar nursery | `#claim/solid` | Yes — nearest large star-forming region, ~1,344 ly. The "portal" reading is `#claim/speculation`. |
| C11 | The band's glow could be higher-realm energy bleeding through | `#claim/misreading` as physics | The band is resolved starlight + gas + dust. Galileo resolved it into stars in 1610 (*Sidereus Nuncius*); Gaia has cataloged ~1.8 billion of them individually, with parallaxes and proper motions. No unexplained luminosity component exists in the band's photometric budget. This is the finding that kills Version A. |
| C12 | The "glowing center" we see is the galactic center | `#claim/misreading` | The actual center is hidden behind ~30 magnitudes of visual extinction; what we see toward Sagittarius is foreground disk/bulge starlight. We only image the center in infrared/radio/X-ray — and what's there is Sgr A*, a 4-million-solar-mass black hole. Relevant to O8. |
| C13 | (Honest anomaly note) Is there *any* unexplained galactic emission? | `#claim/open` | The Fermi bubbles and the Galactic Center GeV excess are real, actively researched signals with candidate astrophysical explanations (past Sgr A* activity; millisecond pulsars). If the hypothesis ever wants a physical toehold, this is the only honest place to look — logged so nobody pretends the anomaly hunt wasn't done. |

## Scripture, history, mysticism (Sections 2, 3, 4, 5, 6)

| # | Claim | Verdict | Notes |
|---|-------|---------|-------|
| C14 | Isaiah 45:18 — Earth formed to be inhabited; Psalm 19:1 — heavens declare God's glory | `#claim/solid` | Texts say what's claimed. Applying them to galactic placement is `#claim/interpretation`. |
| C15 | Orion named in Job 9:9, 38:31, Amos 5:8 | `#claim/solid` w/ caveat | Hebrew *kesil*; the Orion identification is traditional and standard in translations, but not certain (see [[Scripture Index]]). |
| C16 | Egyptian Sah = celestial Osiris; pharaoh's soul ascends to Orion | `#claim/solid` | Pyramid Texts; mainstream Egyptology. |
| C17 | Giza pyramids deliberately mirror Orion's Belt (Orion Correlation Theory) | `#claim/contested` (fringe) | Bauval & Gilbert 1994; rejected by most Egyptologists — the correlation requires flipping one map (Krupp's objection) and the Menkaure offset/proportions don't match cleanly. Not load-bearing. |
| C18 | Talmud: Orion's heat balances the Pleiades' cold | `#claim/solid` as citation | Berakhot 58b, on Kimah and Kesil. Report of a text, not a physical claim. |
| C19 | "Stretches out the heavens" is a repeated biblical image | `#claim/solid` | Isa 40:22, 42:5, 44:24; Job 9:8; Ps 104:2; Jer 10:12; Zech 12:1. |
| C20 | Isaiah 64:1 asks God to *tear* the heavens; Mark 1:10 uses *schizō* for the baptism sky | `#claim/solid` | Hebrew *qara*; Greek *schizō*. Matthew/Luke use *anoigō* ("opened") — Mark's word choice is deliberate and violent. |
| C21 | Mark's *schizō* forms an inclusio with the temple veil (15:38), and Josephus says the veil depicted the heavens | `#claim/solid` | Josephus, *Jewish War* 5.212–214; the inclusio argument is published scholarship (Ulansey, *JBL* 110, 1991). **Strongest textual asset — promoted into the [[Steelman]].** |
| C22 | Sky rolls up like a scroll (Rev 6:14, Isa 34:4); "mirrors" GR's dynamic spacetime | Text `#claim/solid`; mirror-claim `#claim/interpretation` | Convergence of imagery, not prediction. |
| C23 | Tzimtzum: God contracts His light to make the void of creation | `#claim/solid` as doctrine | Lurianic Kabbalah. "Matches the exact physics of collapse" is `#claim/interpretation` — and directionally backwards (withdrawal-to-make-room vs concentration-that-seals-off). |
| C24 | Ancient Hebrews pictured a solid raqia holding back waters | `#claim/solid` | Mainstream ANE scholarship (Gen 1:6–8; Job 37:18; comparative Mesopotamian material). Contested by some conservative interpreters — noted. |
| C25 | Milky Way as "Way to Heaven" / road of souls in medieval and ancient folklore | `#claim/solid` | Cicero's Dream of Scipio + Macrobius (souls' road via the Milky Way), Norse/Baltic/North American parallels, English "Walsingham Way." Genuine cross-cultural folklore. |
| C26 | String theory/brane cosmology: universes as 3D membranes near higher dimensions | `#claim/contested` | Real published framework (Randall–Sundrum etc.), speculative and unconfirmed. Theological application is `#claim/speculation`. |

## Audit summary
- **Retired outright:** C8 (Jude→black holes), C11–C12 as physics (they falsify Version A).
- **Demoted to non-load-bearing:** C5, C6 (weakened forms only), C17, C26.
- **Promoted:** C21 — the Mark/Josephus veil material is stronger than the original foundation realized.
- **Left open on purpose:** C13 — the anomaly ledger, so the physical question stays honest instead of quietly assumed either way.
