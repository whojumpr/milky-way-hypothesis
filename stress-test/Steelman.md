# Steelman — the strongest surviving version

What remains after [[Claims Audit]] and [[Objections Ledger]], stated as strongly as the evidence allows. This is Version C of [[The Hypothesis]], with Version B held alongside as declared metaphysics.

## The claim

**The Milky Way is not a tear in the sky; it is the sign of one.** The heavens function, as Psalm 19 says, as declaration — and the specific thing the band has declared to nearly every human culture that ever looked up is *a luminous path between this world and another*. That reading is not an accident of one mythology; it recurs independently (Rome's road of souls, the Norse and Baltic paths, the medieval "Way to Heaven," the dome-seam intuition of the ancient Near East). The band is a real structure whose form follows necessarily from our real address, and whose effect on human perception has been, as a matter of record, to point beyond the world.

## The four load-bearing supports (all `#claim/solid`)

1. **The address is real** (C1–C4). We are embedded in the disk of a spiral galaxy, in the Local Arm between Sagittarius and Perseus, ~26,700 ly from the center; the band is the necessary appearance of that address. Nothing here is contested. (Round-1 correction absorbed: our arm is major-arm-scale, not a "minor spur" — which costs the *design* argument a premise but costs this support nothing; the address itself is untouched.)

2. **The tearing pattern in scripture is real and deliberate** (C19–C21). The heavens as stretched fabric is one of scripture's most repeated cosmological images; Isaiah prays for the heavens to be *torn*; and Mark brackets his entire gospel with exactly two uses of *schizō* — the sky ripped at the baptism, the temple veil ripped at the death — where Josephus tells us that veil was itself embroidered with the panorama of the heavens. In Mark's own architecture, **the torn veil is a torn sky**: divine entry is marked by tearing the barrier. This is published scholarship, not numerology.

3. **The path-of-souls reception is real** (C25, C27, O9). Across unconnected cultures the band was read as the road between worlds. For a *sign* hypothesis this reception history is not trivia — it is the evidence. A sign is verified by what it reliably signifies to its readers. The hardest artifact in this support is the Great Pyramid's star shafts (C27): a civilization that physically built its soul-launch apparatus aimed at Orion and the imperishable stars — reception history in stone, not just in story.

4. **The address is favorable, and legible** (C5–C6, weakened forms). Our placement permits life and permits astronomy — put forward not as a design *proof* (O7 stands; the anthropic objection is live, the rarity premise weakened by round 1) but as *consonance*: the facts sit comfortably with Isaiah 45:18 and Psalm 19:1, which is all a typological claim needs them to do. This is deliberately the weakest of the four supports, and the steelman does not lean on it.

5. **The located gate, personalized** (C28–C30; round 2 — `#claim/interpretation` with solid subcomponents). The Bible's own paradigm of a located breach is Bethel: a hapax structure bridging earth and heaven with two-way angelic traffic, terror fixed to the *place*, an anointed marker-stone, and the explicit label "**gate of heaven**" — a scene with recognized Mesopotamian cosmic-stairway resonance (*simmiltu*; Winitzer 2025) — which John 1:51 then relocates **from a place onto a person**, using *anoigō* where Mark alone keeps Isaiah's torn (*schizō*/*qaraʿ*) heavens. And the reception history closes the loop back to the band itself: medieval Europe named the Milky Way the Way of St. James / Jakobsweg — pilgrim-road names carrying a souls-to-paradise legend, drawn by the Iacobus conflation toward the patriarch of the ladder (Orion's Belt as "Jacob's Staff" beside the road). Honest limits, recorded: Gen 28 contains no Milky Way and its imagery is a *gate*, not a rip — Bethel is the structural cousin of the Markan tear, not the same image; and the band-to-Ladder link specifically is contested (one uncited Allen sentence).

## What this version explicitly does not claim
- Not that any photon in the band is unexplained (O1–O3 conceded in full).
- Not that Jude, the Abyss, or tzimtzum anticipated black holes (O5 conceded; C8 retired).
- Not that the Giza correlation or the Orion-portal material carries weight (C17, C10 demoted to color).
- Not that Version B (dimensional overlap) is scientifically supported — it is held, if at all, as declared metaphysics with no borrowed credibility (O6).

## Why this is stronger, not weaker
The original hypothesis staked itself on physics it could only lose. The steelman stakes itself on three things that are documented — the astronomy of our address, the literary architecture of tearing in scripture, and the cross-cultural reception of the band as the road to Heaven — and on one live philosophical debate (fine-tuning) where it takes the defensible side of an open question rather than a closed one. Every objection in the ledger either misses it or feeds it.
