# Steelman — the strongest surviving version

What remains after [[Claims Audit]], [[Objections Ledger]], four research rounds, and review round 1. This is Version C of [[The Hypothesis]]. **The claim itself is `#claim/interpretation` — a Christian theological reading built on documented inputs.** The structure below keeps the documented inputs, the non-load-bearing patterns, and the theological inference strictly separated, per [[Rules of Evidence]].

## The claim (`#claim/interpretation`)

**The Milky Way is not a tear in the sky; it is read here as the sign of one.** The heavens function, as Psalm 19 says, as declaration — and one of the recurring things the band has signified across several independent traditions is *a luminous path between this world and another*. The band is a real structure whose appearance follows necessarily from our real address; the reading of it as a designed sign is this project's theological interpretation, not an audit result.

## Documented inputs (solid observations)

1. **The address is real** (C1–C4, `#claim/solid`). We are embedded in the disk of a spiral galaxy, in the Local Arm between the Sagittarius and Perseus arms, ~26,700 ly from the galactic center; the band is the necessary appearance of that address. (Round-1 correction absorbed: our arm is major-arm-scale, not a "minor spur.")

2. **The tearing texts are real** (C19–C21). Documented: the stretched-heavens formula across seven-plus OT texts; Isaiah 64:1's *qāraʿ* ("tear the heavens"); Mark's exactly two uses of *schizō* (1:10 baptism sky, 15:38 temple veil) — the only "torn heavens" in the NT, where the parallels use *anoigō*; Josephus' description of the outer temple veil as an image of the heavens (*War* 5.212–214) and Philo's veil-as-cosmos allegory (*QE* 2.85, 2.91; *Mos.* 2.88). **Interpretive layer, labeled as such:** reading the two tearings as a deliberate cosmic inclusio — "the torn veil is a torn sky" — is Ulansey's published interpretation (*JBL* 110/1, 1991), attractive and defended here, but the veil's referent (inner vs. outer curtain) remains debated in scholarship, and "Matthew and Luke soften Mark" is redactional interpretation, not fact.

3. **The band was read as the road of the dead in several independent traditions** (C25, C32; scoped by round 4's [[Global Milky Way Survey]]). In our surveyed corpus, 15 of 34 well-attested traditions were coded as involving soul- or otherworld-travel on the band, collapsing to roughly four mutually independent lineages: the classical Mediterranean (Heraclides fr. 52 Schütrumpf; Cicero, *Somn.* 6.16 — the Milky Circle as the dwelling of the blessed; Macrobius and Porphyry supplying the two-gate doctrine, C32, Latin verified); the Balto-Finnic belt (Estonian archive material; Kuperjanov 2001); a family of Indigenous North and Mesoamerican traditions each attested in its own right — Lakota (*Wanáǧi Tȟačháŋku*, Powers 1975), Ojibwe (*jiibay-miikana*, Baraga 1878), Skidi Pawnee (Chamberlain 1982), Cheyenne and Apache (Curtis 1907/1911), Paiute (Mooney 1896), Seneca (Wallace 1972), K'iche' Maya (*xibalba be*, Tedlock), with the Mississippian "Path of Souls" as a peer-reviewed iconographic reconstruction (Lankford 2007) — whose degree of shared inheritance is a scholarly hypothesis, not a settled fact; and the Yolŋu people of Arnhem Land (Wells 1964; the Milŋiyawuy river of stars — living knowledge of a living community, cited with that standing). This is a strong plurality, not a universal: independent traditions in the Andes, East and South Asia, southern Africa, Polynesia, and most of Siberia read the band otherwise. Each tradition's own destination and terminology is preserved in the survey — "path between worlds" is *our comparative coding*, and resemblance implies no shared theology and no endorsement of Version C by any of these communities. The convergence claim is `#claim/pattern` and carries no evidential weight on its own.

## Non-load-bearing reinforcement (patterns and consonance — explicitly not evidence)

4. **Other sacred-sky comparisons** (`#claim/pattern`). Materials that are *adjacent* to the soul-road reading but are not attestations of it: the rabbinic *nehar di-nur* (a throne-room fire-river that later interpreters, including the Jewish Encyclopedia, identify with the Milky Way — C31, `#claim/interpretation`); Graur's proposal (2024–25, *JAHH*) that the Milky Way manifested aspects of the Egyptian sky-goddess Nut — a recent scholarly hypothesis, not an ancient attestation (C31); the Giza star shafts, whose measured geometry and retrocalculated Orion's Belt / circumpolar alignments are solid but whose deliberate targeting and ritual function remain disputed (C27) — and which concern Orion, not the Milky Way; Theophrastus' seam-of-the-hemispheres theory of the band (via Macrobius, C31) — an ancient Greek physical theory, not a Hebrew or theological one; and the Bethel/John 1:51 "located gate personalized" thread (C28–C30), whose textual subcomponents are solid but whose connection to the band runs through the medieval Way of **Saint James** sky-names — the patriarch-Jacob/Ladder link rests on one uncited 1899 sentence and stays an unresolved suggestion.

5. **The address is consonant with the design texts** (C5–C6 weakened forms; O7 open). Our placement permits life and permits astronomy; Isaiah 45:18 and Psalm 19:1 sit comfortably with that. This is consonance, not evidence: the anthropic objection stands (O7), the rarity premise weakened in round 1, and cosmological fine-tuning (a live philosophical debate — see the SEP fine-tuning entry) is a *separate* argument from any claim about our particular galactic address. *The Privileged Planet* is an intelligent-design argument and is cited as such. Nothing here carries weight.

## What this version explicitly does not claim
- Not that any photon in the band is unexplained (O1–O3 conceded: known astrophysical populations adequately account for the observed band; no robust anomalous optical component is detected).
- Not that Jude, the Abyss, or tzimtzum anticipated black holes (O5 conceded; C8 retired).
- Not that the ground-plan Orion Correlation Theory or the Orion-portal material carries weight (C17, C10 — color only).
- Not that Version B (dimensional overlap) is scientifically supported — it is held, if at all, as declared metaphysics with no borrowed credibility (O6).
- Not that the cross-cultural pattern, the gematria threads, or any `#claim/pattern` item is evidence of anything (Rules of Evidence: patterns never carry weight).

## Status
The claim is a theological interpretation whose *inputs* survived four research rounds and a full review round: the astronomy of our address is uncontested; the tearing texts and their two independent first-century veil-witnesses are documented; the soul-road reception history is real at its honestly counted size. The objections ledger contains no unanswered defeater of Version C — and Version C, correctly understood, makes no claim the sciences could defeat.
