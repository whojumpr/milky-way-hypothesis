# The Milky Way Hypothesis

An Obsidian vault stress-testing one idea: **that the Milky Way is a tear in the fabric of the heavens through which the light of Heaven leaks into our world** — where it came from, what the evidence does to it, and which version of it survives.

This is not an apologetics project and not a debunking project. The original hypothesis is preserved intact in `foundation/`; the stress-test layer (`stress-test/`) attacks it at full strength; and the result is a smaller, harder claim that actually stands ([`Steelman`](stress-test/Steelman.md)).

## Structure
- **`Home.md`** — map of contents; start here (open the folder as a vault in Obsidian)
- **`The Hypothesis.md`** — the claim split into its three versions: physical (A), metaphysical (B), typological (C)
- **`foundation/`** — the original six sections: our galactic address, the designed-address argument, Orion's spiritual history, space-as-fabric in scripture, black holes as spiritual paradigms, and the heaven-leaking-through synthesis
- **`stress-test/`** — the ringer: a claims audit (every factual assertion checked and tagged), an objections ledger (nine objections at full strength, with statuses), and the steelman
- **`sources/`** — scripture index with original-language notes
- **`Rules of Evidence.md`** — the claim-tag taxonomy and the stress-test protocol
- **`Research Log.md` / `CHANGELOG.md`** — every session and every change

## Current state of the hypothesis
- **Version A** (literal physical tear): retired — three independent fatal objections (the photon budget is closed; the band is disk geometry, a great circle, not a seam; resolved into stars since 1610).
- **Version B** (dimensional overlap): held as explicit metaphysics; unfalsifiable, so barred from borrowing scientific support.
- **Version C** (the band as designed sign — the tearing pattern of Mark's *schizō* inclusio, the heavens-embroidered temple veil in Josephus, the cross-cultural path-of-souls reception): **alive**, with its open problems listed honestly.

## Working rules
1. Never commit credentials — no keys, tokens, or `.env` files, in any commit.
2. Every commit is approved before it happens.
3. Every task is logged (`Research Log.md`) and every landed change recorded (`CHANGELOG.md`).
4. Claims keep their evidence tags; nothing contested or speculative gets used as load-bearing support.
