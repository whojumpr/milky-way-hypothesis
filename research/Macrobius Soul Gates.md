# The Classical Soul-Gates — Macrobius, Porphyry, Numenius (round 3)

Researched 2026-08-25, primary texts verified (Macrobius in Latin, Eyssenhardt Teubner; Porphyry in Taylor's translation). Feeds [[Claims Audit]] C32 and upgrades [[Steelman]] support 3.

## 1. Macrobius, *Commentary on the Dream of Scipio* 1.11.12–1.12.18 — verified in the Latin
**1.12.1:** the Milky Way girdles the zodiac, crossing it "at the two tropical signs, Capricorn and Cancer" — *has solis portas physici vocaverunt*, "natural philosophers named these the portals of the sun." **1.12.2:** *per has portas animae de caelo in terras meare et de terris in caelum remeare creduntur* — through these gates souls pass between heaven and earth; **Cancer is the portal of men** (descent into the lower world), **Capricorn the portal of gods** (return to immortality). **1.12.3–4:** Pythagoras — the empire of Dis begins downward from the Milky Way; souls still in Cancer "have not yet left the Milky Way and are still numbered among the gods"; Macrobius explicitly hooks this to Cicero's *Somnium* ("setting out from here, they return here"). Then the full itinerary: Leo → the Crater (first draught of matter, forgetting/Lethe) → seven planetary vestings (Saturn: reason; Jupiter: action; Mars: spiritedness; Sun: perception; Venus: desire; Mercury: interpretation; Moon: growth) → the body. He credits *physici*, Homer (the Ithacan cave's two doors), Pythagoras, the Orphics, Plato — **not** Numenius by name in 1.12.

## 2. Porphyry, *De antro nympharum* — the earlier statement
Chs. 21–23 (Taylor, verified verbatim): the cave as image of the cosmos "**as Numenius and his familiar Cronius assert**"; "theologists say that Cancer is the gate through which souls descend, but Capricorn that through which they ascend" — the gates defined by the **tropics**. **The Milky Way enters explicitly in ch. 28:** "Capricorn and Cancer are situated about the galaxy, being allotted the extremities of this circle… According to Pythagoras, the people of dreams are the souls which are said to be collected in the galaxy, this circle being so called from the milk with which souls are nourished when they fall into generation" — hence funerary libations of milk and honey. Ch. 24: Mithras enthroned at the equinoxes between the gates. Ch. 29: a variant scheme (Sun and Moon as the gates) — Porphyry compiles variants, not one dogma.

## 3. How much is genuinely Numenian?
Attested for Numenius (mid-2nd c. AD) through **two independent channels**: Porphyry (frr. 31–32 des Places: cave-cosmos + galaxy/milk) and Proclus (*In Remp.* — fr. 35: Numenius located the chasms of Plato's Myth of Er at Capricorn and Cancer, citing Homer's "gates of the sun" and the "people of dreams"). The tidy integrated itinerary with the faculty-catalogue is plausibly Macrobius' (or his Porphyrian source's) synthesis — the catalogue's precise form appears first in Macrobius. Scholarly split on how much of 1.12 is Numenius (Dodds yes; Beutler doubted) — recorded.

## 4. The astronomy check — the doctrine is wrong by one sign, and that's the finding
The Milky Way actually crosses the zodiac in **Taurus–Gemini** and **Scorpius–Sagittarius/Ophiuchus** — one sign from Cancer/Capricorn. Stahl's own footnote flags it, and **a medieval glossator on Bede's *De natura rerum* already corrected Macrobius**. Roger Beck (*Studies in Mithraism*, 1994, p. 50 n. 79): the two authors "naturally wished" the two soul-roads — solstitial gates and galactic path — to meet; "a modern believer in such matters would not have to stretch the evidence, for the solstices, as a result of precession, are nowadays very close to the points where the galactic equator intersects the ecliptic." Precession caveats, stated precisely: the *sign-names* fossilize the epoch when solstices fell in Cancer/Capricorn, but **the galactic crossings were never in those constellations in any epoch** — the solstice–galaxy coincidence is best *now* (~1998–2012), not in 2000 BC. Do not use the "fossilized doctrine" rescue for the Milky Way component (*Hamlet's Mill*'s view — minority, logged).

**Why the error is load-bearing for the vault:** the gate-reading was so theologically necessary that it overrode the visible sky by a full sign and survived a millennium of correction as standard cosmology. Antiquity did not merely admire the band — it read the band's intersections with the sun's road as the literal **seams where the cosmos opens**, one door down into incarnation, one door up to the gods.

## 5. Wider echoes
- **Heraclides Ponticus (4th c. BC), earliest explicit attestation:** Empedotimus' vision — "the Milky Way is the path of souls passing through the Hades in the heaven" (frs. 96–98 Wehrli; exact fragment page unverified — flag). Antedates Cicero by three centuries.
- **Mithraism:** Ulansey, *The Origins of the Mithraic Mysteries* (Oxford 1989) builds on *De antro*'s gate cosmology. **⚠ DEPENDENCY LOGGED: this is the same David Ulansey as the vault's Mark-inclusio article (JBL 110/1, 1991). The tear imagery (C21) and the Mithraic gate-cosmology witness are one scholar — count as one witness, not two.** (The Macrobius/Porphyry primary texts stand independently of him.)
- **Celsus' Mithraic ladder:** Origen, *Contra Celsum* 6.21–22 — the seven-gated planetary ladder of soul-ascent; the same cosmology in cultic form (and a ladder, linking to [[Jacobs Ladder Reception]]).
- **Cult practice:** milk-and-honey libations to the dead tied to the galaxy-milk doctrine (*De antro* 28) — doctrine embedded in ritual, not just philosophy.
- Lead only (unverified): a Salona epitaph, "From Salona to the Milky Way."
