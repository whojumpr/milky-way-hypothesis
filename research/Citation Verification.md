## The correction (C27 attribution)
- **Badawy & Trimble 1964** (*Mitteilungen des Instituts für Orientforschung* 10 — Trimble pp. 183–187, Badawy pp. 189–206, using Petrie's survey data): **King's Chamber shafts only** — southern shaft → **Orion's Belt at culmination** ca. 2600 BC (the Belt generally, not Alnitak specifically); northern shaft → circumpolar region / **Thuban (α Draconis)**.
- **Bauval 1993–94** (*Discussions in Egyptology* 26 & 27, 1993; *The Orion Mystery*, 1994 — enabled by **Gantenbrink's 1993 robot survey**, the first precise measurement of the Queen's Chamber shaft angles): **Queen's south → Sirius**, Queen's north → Kochab, and the Alnitak-specific refinement of the King's southern shaft.
- Consequence for the audit: the 1964, mainstream-adjacent core of C27 (King's shafts: Orion's Belt + circumpolar stars, ritual stellar destination) **stands and is what carries the weight**; the Sirius/Alnitak specifics inherit Bauval's more contested provenance and are demoted to supporting detail. Popular pages folding all four stars into 1964 are wrong — do not cite them.
