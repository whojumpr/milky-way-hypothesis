# C13 — The Anomaly Ledger (research round 1)

Status dossier on every candidate "unexplained" galactic emission, researched 2026-08-25. Kept per [[Rules of Evidence]]: no inflating, no deflating beyond the literature. Feeds [[Claims Audit]] C13.

## 1. Fermi bubbles (and eROSITA bubbles)
Two giant gamma-ray lobes ~25,000 ly above/below the plane (Su, Slatyer & Finkbeiner 2010, ApJ 724, 1044), enclosed by even larger soft-X-ray bubbles found by eROSITA (Predehl et al. 2020, Nature 588, 227) — together implying a ~10^56 erg energy injection from the galactic center. Competing **conventional** origins: a Sgr A* jet outburst ~2.6 Myr ago (Yang, Ruszkowski & Zweibel 2022, Nature Astronomy), a nuclear starburst wind, or recurrent tidal disruption events (A&A 2025). The emission mechanism itself (cosmic rays in shocked plasma) is not mysterious — only *which historical event* inflated them.
**Status: explained with competing models, not unexplained.**

## 2. Galactic Center GeV excess
The ~1–3 GeV diffuse excess (Goodenough & Hooper 2009). The 2024–2026 evidence has moved toward the mundane explanation: Manconi et al. 2025 (PRD, arXiv:2511.03350) find the excess traces the *stellar bulge*, not a dark-matter-like profile; an ML source-classification study (PRD 111, 043033, 2025) finds an unresolved millisecond-pulsar population sufficient to explain it; new bulge MSPs are being found directly. The DM reading is narrowed but alive (Zhong et al. 2024, PRD 109, 123017; PRL 2025 on non-spherical DM morphology).
**Status: competing models; the conventional one (MSPs) has gained ground.**

## 3. Other claimed anomalies
- **511 keV positron line (bulge):** source unattributed after ~50 years (Prantzos et al. 2011, RMP 83, 1001; 20-year INTEGRAL/SPI legacy map, A&A 2025), but every production channel on offer is ordinary astrophysics (supernova nucleosynthesis, X-ray binaries, MSPs, transport effects). COSI should discriminate. **Unidentified source, non-anomalous physics.**
- **ARCADE-2 / radio synchrotron background:** diffuse radio background ~3–10× brighter than summed known sources — the closest thing to "genuinely unexplained" on the ledger. But it is an **all-sky background** (plausibly extragalactic or a foreground/calibration systematic), not Milky Way band emission. **Unresolved; not a feature of the band.**
- **3.5 keV X-ray line:** substantially deflated — Dessert, Rodd & Safdi 2020 (Science) against the DM-decay reading; XRISM's 2025 ten-cluster stack finds **no unidentified lines**. **An anomaly in the process of dissolving.**
- **WMAP/Planck haze:** now identified with the Fermi-bubble electron population — folded into item 1, not independent.

## 4. Bottom line for the hypothesis
**Nothing on this ledger is emission with no viable astrophysical explanation.** And decisive for the retired Version A: every item lives in gamma rays, X-rays, MeV lines, or radio — **none is optical**. Even if one turned exotic tomorrow, it would not perturb the visible band's closed photon budget and would not resurrect "the band's light leaks from another realm."

## Verdict applied
C13 stays `#claim/open` with an updated one-liner in [[Claims Audit]]. Full source list preserved below.
