# Second Temple Ascent Literature — Heaven's Architecture Between Bethel and the Rabbis (round 4)

Researched 2026-08-25. Fills the gap between Gen 28 / the OT and the rabbinic *nehar di-nur*. Feeds [[Claims Audit]] C33 and strengthens C21.

## 1. 1 Enoch — the earliest built-out heaven
- **The Book of the Watchers (3rd c. BCE), chs. 14:** Enoch ascends *along the courses of stars and lightning* to a wall "built of crystals and surrounded by tongues of fire," then two nested houses — the first crystal, **its ceiling "like the path of the stars and the lightnings"** (14:11, 17), the second of flame, holding the crystal throne with **"streams of flaming fire" from beneath it** (14:19) — the earliest elaboration of what Daniel 7:10 renders as the fire-river (1 En 14 is generally dated *earlier* than Daniel 7). Consensus reading (Nickelsburg's Hermeneia; Himmelfarb): the three-zone structure is **a heavenly temple** — courtyard, hekhal, Holy of Holies.
- **Chs. 33–36:** twelve portals at the ends of the earth where the firmament meets the rim — winds, dew, and stars pass through physical apertures; **chs. 72–82 (Astronomical Book):** the sun rises and sets through six eastern and six western gates, one per month. Ratzon (*DSD* 22, 2015): the gates are real cosmological structures — horizon openings, a Judaized transform of Babylonian astronomy. **The sky has literal doors, and astronomy is the science of the doors.**
- **No Milky Way anywhere in 1 Enoch.** The one fire-river on the cosmic tour (17:5) is identified by scholarship (Coblentz Bautch 2003) with the *underworld* Pyriphlegethon — the identification points down, not up.

## 2. The other ascents (each verified)
- **Testament of Levi 2–3, 5:** "the heavens were opened"; **waters hanging between the heavens**; "in the highest of all dwelleth the Great Glory, **in the holy of holies**"; "the angel opened to me **the gates of heaven**, and I saw the holy temple." (Cite as three-heavens-later-expanded-to-seven — the MS tradition wavers.)
- **2 Enoch:** stacked heavens; the sun's twelve gates relocated *inside* the fourth heaven; the zodiac in the eighth — proof these authors could name celestial features, **and still never named the galaxy**.
- **3 Baruch:** five heavens entered through huge gates; Baruch cannot pass the fifth gate until Michael descends **with the keys** — and never reaches the throne. Heaven as a gated building with an inaccessible top floor.
- **Ascension of Isaiah:** seven heavens, a throne in each; the Beloved *descends* through them incognito, transforming at each gate.
- **Apocalypse of Abraham:** ascent **through a fiery firmament** — the boundary itself is fire; the ascent dangerous; a hymn as protection.
- **The frame — Himmelfarb, *Ascent to Heaven* (Oxford 1993):** heaven is understood as a **temple**, from the Book of the Watchers onward; the ascending **seer functions as a priest** entering it.

## 3. Hekhalot / Merkabah — the rivers of fire multiplied
Scholem (*Major Trends*, Lecture 2): seven palaces (*hekhalot*) within the seventh heaven, each gate held by terrifying angelic gatekeepers, passed with the correct **seal**; the ascent mortally dangerous. **The nehar di-nur is emphatically multiplied** (Sefer Hekhalot / 3 Enoch, Odeberg trans., archive.org): **four rivers of fire** under the Ḥayyot flanking the Throne; **seven** when the judgment throne is set; the named river **Rigyon**, formed **from the sweat of the fiery creatures, who perspire out of dread of God's majesty**, with its own appointed angel — explicitly proof-texting Daniel 7:10. Daniel's single stream becomes permanent throne-room infrastructure. **Never mapped onto the visible sky in any passage found.** (Schäfer's specific wording unverified this session — cite Scholem + primary texts.)

## 4. Temple cosmology — the two-way claim
- **Philo (verified; sections corrected round 4):** *QE* 2.85 — the ten curtains' four materials = the four elements (read in Marcus's Loeb, archive.org); ***QE* 2.91 — the veil proper as the divide between the changeable sublunary region and the unchanging heavenly one** (the passage to hang "veil = heavens" on); *Mos.* 2.88 — tabernacle = universe, lampstand = seven planets. **Second independent first-century witness beside Josephus (*War* 5.212–214) for the veil as woven cosmos — upgrades C21's foundation and dilutes the single-witness concern about Ulansey (the primary texts now corroborate independently).**
- **Beale, *The Temple and the Church's Mission* (2004):** courtyard = earth/sea, Holy Place = visible heavens, **Holy of Holies = the invisible heaven of God's presence** — the widely accepted tripartite cosmos-map (ANE temples as microcosms).
- **Barker — flagged contested:** her general veil-as-boundary point overlaps with Philo/Josephus and is safe; her First-Temple reconstruction is outside the mainstream. Cite only alongside Philo/Josephus/Beale.
- Himmelfarb upward + Philo/Josephus/Beale downward = one claim in both directions: **heaven is a temple and the temple is a map of heaven.**

## 5. The Milky Way verdict — ABSENT explicitly, THIN as lineage
No Second Temple or hekhalot text names the Milky Way in any form, and no scholarship claims one does. The *nehar di-nur* = Milky Way identification is rabbinic-era, anchored on **Shmuel** (3rd c. Amora, famed astronomer) in b. Berakhot 58b — Scorpio's tail resting in the fire-river, which is *observationally true* of the band: a genuine act of sky-mapping performed retrospectively on Daniel's throne-room image. **Do not back-date it.** One honest "suggestive but unclaimed" item, held as `#claim/pattern`: 1 Enoch 14's fire-streams flow in a room whose ceiling is "the path of the stars" — the ingredients share a room three centuries early, but no ancient author and no modern scholar combines them. (A Forward "Philologos" column directly on the Daniel/Milky Way question exists but returned 403 — existence citable, argument not.)

## What this adds
The corpus is the span between Bethel and Mark — **periodized per review round 1: Second Temple texts (1 Enoch, T. Levi), post-70 apocalypses (3 Baruch, Ascension of Isaiah, Apoc. Abraham), and much later hekhalot mysticism are distinct corpora, not one "Second Temple Judaism"** — across which Bethel's "gate of heaven" grows into a full architecture: horizon-gates for the luminaries, stacked heavens with locked doors, guarded palace-gates with seals. **Several texts depict materialized boundaries** — crystal wall, woven veil, fiery firmament (varied materials, not one fabric). Mark's torn veil / torn heavens lands on a tradition-stream that had long specified what a boundary between the worlds is made of.
