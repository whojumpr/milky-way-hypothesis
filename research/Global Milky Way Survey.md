Researched 2026-08-25. The full-strength test of the cross-cultural support (C25) and objection O9. ## The honest statistical read
The soul-road is **one motif among several — but far and away the single most mythologically elaborated one**, at ~40–45% of well-attested traditions. Not a majority: river readings, object-origin readings (ashes, straw, cornmeal, milk), and dark-constellation systems collectively equal or exceed it. Distribution: near-universal in indigenous North America, strong in the Balto-Finnic belt, present in the Mediterranean and Arnhem Land — **absent from East/South Asia, the Andes, southern Africa, Polynesia, the Middle East, most of Siberia**.

## Independence analysis — the number that matters
Collapsing for plausible transmission, independent inventions reduce to **~4**:
1. **Mediterranean/Near East** (Greco-Roman → medieval Europe → plausibly rabbinic via Hellenistic contact — one lineage, not three);
2. **Balto-Finnic/Volga belt** (one areal complex per Berezkin);
3. **The Americas** (best counted as *one* deep Beringian tradition, magnificently preserved across a dozen attested nations — Berezkin documents the Siberian–American continuities);
4. **Yolngu Australia** (no plausible transmission).
Four independent inventions across the world's macro-regions is real convergence — at the scale of *recurring human inference*, not *universal sign*. Equally independent regions built no soul-road from the same sky.

## Notable per-tradition anchors (all sourced in the dossier)
- Lakota: the band's light as "the campfires of ghosts on the road" (Powers 1975: 53, 93) — the same image as Yolngu Arnhem Land, independently.
- Maya: the *dark rift* is the underworld road — the road is the band's darkness, not its light. A reading the vault should sit with.
- Siberia's dominant reading is a *ski track* from the sky-elk hunt (Berezkin) — the often-hand-waved "Siberian shamanic soul-road" is largely a miss.
- Honesty note on our own holdings: *nehar di-nur* and Nut are formally *river* and *body* readings with afterlife associations — they support "the band is otherworld-charged," not "the band is a road."

## Verdicts applied
- **The convergence claim: `#claim/pattern`** — genuine, survives the honest count, but is a strong plurality resting on ~4 independent lineages, not a cross-cultural constant.
- **O9: upheld as a valid scope restriction, not a refutation** — [[Objections Ledger]] updated.
- **[[Steelman]] support 3 re-worded** to the four-lineage formulation with the misses named.
