# C24 — The Solid Raqia Debate, Both Sides (round 3)

Researched 2026-08-25. Feeds [[Claims Audit]] C24/C31 and forces a correction to [[06 Heaven Leaking Through]].

## 1. The case FOR the solid raqia (mainstream)
Seely, "The Firmament and the Water Above," *WTJ* 53 (1991) 227–40 (Part I read in full): the solid reading is in the standard lexica and the spine of critical commentary (Driver, Gunkel, Skinner, von Rad, Westermann). Four moves: all ancient peoples took the sky as solid; grammar (stars *in* the raqia, birds *in front of* it; *raqaʿ* = hammer out, cf. Exod 39:3; Job 37:18 "hard as a molten mirror"); function (horizontal dam holding the heavenly ocean — Gen 7:11; Ps 148:4); Ezek 1:22's crystal raqia with a throne on it. BDB s.v. רקיע sense 2 states the solid reading outright (conceded even by Poythress). LXX *stereōma* / Vulgate *firmamentum*. The reading is now inside the evangelical academy too (Greenwood, IVP 2015; Enns at BioLogos). **Walton nuance (do not misuse):** *Genesis 1 as Ancient Cosmology* (2011) relocates the solid sky lexically from *raqia* to *šĕḥāqîm* — he still affirms the Hebrews held a solid sky; he is not a witness against it.

## 2. The case AGAINST (minority, chiefly confessional)
Younker & Davidson, "The Myth of the Solid Heavenly Dome," *AUSS* 49.1 (2011) 125–47: the dome-plus-flat-earth picture owes much to 19th-century construction (Irving's Columbus fiction, Letronne, pan-Babylonianism); no cuneiform "vault" (Jensen's 1890 mistranslation); *raqia* never associated with metal; Job 37:18's "mirror" (*rĕʾî*, hapax) repointable to "appearance." Poythress, *WTJ* 77 (2015) 181–91 (read in full): Gen 1:6–8 is phenomenal language; Israelite farmers knew rain came from clouds (Judg 5:4; 1 Kgs 18:44–45); "windows of heaven" are house-imagery — a literal sluice would give a river, not forty days of rain; and the heavenly-sea reading is itself an anachronism, a "quasi-scientific" theory imposed on the text. Their strongest ground: the *dome shape*; their weakness: Horowitz's stone heavens cut against the *shape*, not against *solidity* — both camps use the same data, and the vault must not cite Horowitz as anti-solidity.

## 3. The comparative data
- **Mesopotamia (Horowitz, *Mesopotamian Cosmic Geography*):** KAR 307's three superimposed **stone** heavens — *luludānītu*-stone (Anu's), *saggilmud*-stone (Marduk enthroned in a lapis cella), **jasper** lower heavens "of the stars, with the constellations drawn on its surface." Solid — but flat floors, not a dome. Biblical resonances: Ezek 1:22; Exod 24:10's lapis pavement.
- **Egypt:** sky as Nut over the waters of Nun; the sky's *material* genuinely disputed among Egyptologists (Allen: an "interface" of the waters; Lesko: solid canopy; Conman: moving dome) — Egypt arms both sides.

## 4. Where the field stands (2010s–2020s)
**Solid raqia: still the working consensus of critical scholarship**, with real but minority dissent concentrated in confessional venues; not displaced in the lexica or major commentaries. **The genuine refinement is shape, not substance:** careful current treatments say solid, probably *flat*, possibly one of several stacked heavens — "crystalline dome" specifically is the weakest part of the picture.

## 5. The Milky Way ↔ raqia sub-question — the round's key finding
**ABSENT:** no ANE or early Jewish text connects the Milky Way to the raqia, a seam in the firmament, or the windows of heaven (*arubbot hashamayim* — those discharge water; the rabbinic sun-windows are for the sun). **The vault's "ancients read the band as a seam in the dome" image is a modern imaginative reconstruction with no ancient attestation — corrected in [[06 Heaven Leaking Through]] and tagged accordingly.**

**THIN but real — what the ancients actually did with the band:**
- **Rabbinic *nehar di-nur*** ("river of fire," from Dan 7:10): a standing celestial feature — b. Ḥagigah 14a (angels created daily from it); **b. Berakhot 58b** (Shmuel: Scorpio's sting sits in the river of fire — and Scorpius's tail really does lie in the galactic band, giving the identification astronomical teeth). Jewish Encyclopedia, "Astronomy": "The Milky Way is called 'Fire-Stream'… (Nehar di-nur)." Same Talmud page as our Kimah/Kesil passage (C18).
- **Egyptian Nut:** Graur, *JAHH* 27.1 (2024) 28–45 (+ arXiv:2409.10265) — the Milky Way's seasonal orientations as manifestations of the sky-goddess's body. Peer-reviewed, current.
- **Not usable:** Milky Way = Tiamat's tail (Enuma Elish V) is a modern conjecture (Langdon 1923; Papke 1989), disputed, not text-explicit.

## Verdicts applied
- **C24 stays `#claim/solid`** *as a claim about scholarship* (it is worded sociologically and that wording is sound), re-worded with the two cautions (flat-not-dome; the Walton nuance).
- **New C31:** ancient integration of the band into cosmology — *nehar di-nur* and Nut `#claim/solid` as attestations; the seam-in-the-dome reading `#claim/interpretation`, explicitly **unattested**.
- Net effect on the hypothesis: the historical-origin story in Section 6 *weakens* (no ancient actually recorded the seam reading), while the reception-history support *strengthens* (a fiery river between worlds in the Talmud; a goddess's body in Egypt — the band was integrated into sacred cosmology, just not as a crack).
