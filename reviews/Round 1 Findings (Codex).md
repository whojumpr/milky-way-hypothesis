# Pre-publication Review — Round 1 Findings (Codex, max reasoning, 2026-08-25)

Verbatim findings 1–33 from the round-1 reviewer. Dispositions in [[Review Round 1 Disposition]].

1. **[P1 blocking] — `stress-test/Steelman.md` / “four load-bearing supports”** — The section actually lists five supports, and they are not “all `#claim/solid`”: support 3’s convergence is `#claim/pattern`, support 4 relies on contested C5–C6, and support 5 is explicitly `#claim/interpretation`. This directly violates the rules that patterns “never carry weight” and contested claims cannot be load-bearing. **Fix:** replace the list with a table separating solid observations, non-load-bearing patterns, and the final theological inference; tag the designed-sign conclusion itself `#claim/interpretation`.

2. **[P1 blocking] — `README.md`, `The Hypothesis.md`, `stress-test/Steelman.md` / project posture** — “Not an apologetics project” conflicts with “actually stands,” “defensible,” “takes the defensible side,” and “every objection either misses it or feeds it.” Whatever the authors’ intent, this reads as Christian apologetic natural theology presented as an audit result. **Fix:** identify it plainly as “a Christian theological exploration, not a scientific or historical demonstration,” and replace victory-language with neutral status language.

3. **[P1 blocking] — `stress-test/Steelman.md` / opening claim and support 3** — “Nearly every human culture that ever looked up” directly contradicts the vault’s own result of roughly 15/34 traditions, four proposed lineages, and a plurality rather than a majority. “The single most elaborated reading worldwide” also exceeds what a selected 34-tradition corpus can establish. **Fix:** say, “In our surveyed corpus, 15 of 34 traditions were coded as involving dead-soul or otherworld travel,” disclose sampling limits, and use “some traditions,” not “nearly every culture.”

4. **[P1 blocking] — `stress-test/Steelman.md`, C25, O9 / Indigenous and Aboriginal material** — “The indigenous Americas as one deep tradition” collapses many distinct nations into an unproved macro-lineage, while “Yolngu Australia” is similarly overbroad and does not use the community-preferred spelling Yolŋu. **Fix:** name each people/community, source, collector or knowledge holder, date, language, and confidence in historical relationship; prefer community-authored sources and add a public-sharing/provenance review guided by the [AIATSIS ethical framework](https://aiatsis.gov.au/research/ethical-research).

5. **[P1 blocking] — `stress-test/Steelman.md`, `foundation/06 Heaven Leaking Through.md` / cross-cultural semantics** — Calling all cited traditions a “road to Heaven” assimilates ancestral realms, underworlds, spirit islands, and other culture-specific destinations into a Christian category they did not teach. **Fix:** preserve each tradition’s own destination and terminology, call “path between worlds” the authors’ comparative coding, and state explicitly that resemblance does not imply shared theology or endorsement of Version C; Yolŋu material in particular should be treated as living knowledge, as in this [collaborative account of Milŋiyawuy](https://researchers.westernsydney.edu.au/en/publications/celestial-relations-with-and-as-mil%C5%8Biyawuy-the-milky-way-the-rive-4/).

6. **[P1 blocking] — `stress-test/Steelman.md`, `explorations/Gematria Threads.md`, `foundation/06 Heaven Leaking Through.md` / barred claims reintroduced** — The Steelman’s “dome-seam intuition of the ancient Near East” and Gematria’s “dome in which the ancients read the Milky Way as a seam” contradict C31’s explicit finding that no such ancient connection is attested. “Norse” soul-path material also remains in the Steelman and Section 6 despite C25 barring Bifröst = Milky Way. **Fix:** globally remove or label the seam as the project’s modern imaginative reconstruction, and remove Norse from the soul-road list unless a different, independently verified Norse attestation exists.

7. **[P1 blocking] — `The Hypothesis.md`, O1, C11, `README.md` / photon-budget claims** — “Every photon” is accounted for and Gaia catalogued 1.8 billion contributing stars “each with normal spectra, parallax, and proper motion” are false absolutes: Gaia DR3 has about 1.806 billion photometric sources, full astrometry for about 1.46 billion, and spectra only for subsets; the visual band also contains unresolved and diffuse components. [ESA’s Gaia DR3 inventory](https://www.cosmos.esa.int/web/gaia/dr3) does not support the vault’s wording. **Fix:** say known astrophysical populations and radiative processes adequately explain the observed band and no robust anomalous optical component is detected; describe A as unsupported or observationally excluded in its specified anomalous-emission form, not as proof of an exact zero residual.

8. **[P1 blocking] — `README.md`, O1–O3, Objections scoreboard / “three independent fatal objections”** — O3 is the historical beginning of O1’s resolved-starlight evidence, not an independent evidential line. O2 strongly favors disk geometry and makes a coincident seam ad hoc, but it does not logically falsify a seam model unless that model predicted some different geometry. **Fix:** report one decisive astrophysical evidence family plus a separate geometry/parsimony objection, and reserve “falsified” for a quantitatively specified prediction that observations exclude.

9. **[P1 blocking] — C21, `foundation/04 Space as Fabric.md`, `stress-test/Steelman.md` / Mark and the veil** — The two uses of *schizō* and Josephus’s cosmic outer curtain are solid observations, but “Mark’s torn veil is a torn sky” is Ulansey’s interpretation, not a solid scholarly result; Mark’s curtain may be the inner veil, and Philo’s tabernacle allegory does not independently establish which curtain Mark meant. The referent remains debated in scholarship, including the [inner-veil argument](https://brill.com/view/journals/nt/47/4/article-p344_2.xml) and discussions of Ulansey’s outer-veil reading. **Fix:** split C21 into solid textual facts and an `#claim/interpretation` synthesis—“has been read as a cosmic inclusio”—and likewise demote “Matthew and Luke soften it” from fact to redactional interpretation.

10. **[P1 blocking] — `stress-test/Steelman.md` / support 3** — The paragraph pads a Milky-Way soul-road argument with material that is not such evidence: *nehar di-nur* is a fire-river motif, Nut is a modern scholarly association, and the Great Pyramid shafts concern Orion rather than the Milky Way. **Fix:** move these into a clearly non-load-bearing “other sacred-sky comparisons” subsection and leave support 3 containing only explicit attestations of the Milky Way being traversed by souls or the dead.

11. **[P1 blocking] — C27, `foundation/03 The Spiritual Weight of Orion.md`, `stress-test/Steelman.md` / pyramid shafts** — Approximate retrocalculated stellar alignments do not make deliberate targeting or a soul-channel function `#claim/solid`; shaft purpose remains disputed, and “air shaft is a misnomer” or “they don’t ventilate” is not settled. Current scholarship still discusses ventilation, stellar, solar, and other functions as alternatives ([overview of the dispute](https://archives.palarch.nl/index.php/jae/article/view/6706)). **Fix:** tag measured geometry and conditional astronomical calculations solid, deliberate targeting and ritual purpose contested/interpretive, and delete “reception history in stone.”

12. **[P1 blocking] — C31, `sources/Scripture Index.md`, `foundation/06 Heaven Leaking Through.md` / *nehar di-nur*** — Berakhot 58b says Scorpio’s tail rests in the River of Fire; it does not explicitly gloss that river as the Milky Way. The identification is a later astronomical inference, and the relevant River Dinur discussion is at [Ḥagigah 13b](https://www.sefaria.org/Chagigah.13), not 14a; compare [Berakhot 58b](https://www.sefaria.org/Berakhot.58). **Fix:** correct the folio and write, “later interpreters, including the Jewish Encyclopedia, identify this with the Milky Way,” tagged `#claim/interpretation`.

13. **[P1 blocking] — C31, `foundation/06 Heaven Leaking Through.md`, `stress-test/Steelman.md` / Nut** — “Egypt wove [the Milky Way] into the sky-goddess Nut” turns a recent hypothesis into an ancient attestation. Graur’s own abstract says the Milky Way’s Egyptian role remains unclear and presents the Nut association as “one suggestion”; absence of a rebuttal and a favorable press comment do not upgrade it to solid ([paper record and abstract](https://researchportal.port.ac.uk/en/publications/the-ancient-egyptian-personification-of-the-milky-way-as-the-sky-/)). **Fix:** tag the association contested/interpretive and state that Graur proposes a possible relationship, not a one-to-one ancient identification.

14. **[P1 blocking] — C33 / “Second Temple Judaism”** — The entry combines Second Temple texts with post-70 *3 Baruch*, rabbinic material, and much later hekhalot literature, then attributes one architecture to “Second Temple Judaism.” “The boundary is always fabric” is also contradicted by its own crystal-wall and fiery-firmament examples. **Fix:** divide the evidence into Second Temple, early rabbinic, and late-antique mystical corpora; write “several texts depict materialized boundaries”; and replace “the Milky Way appears nowhere” with “no explicit Milky Way identification was found in the surveyed texts.”

15. **[P1 blocking] — `sources/Scripture Index.md` / verification status** — “Verify wording against a critical text before anything goes public” contradicts the README’s claim that primary sources are verified and publicly advertises unfinished citation work. **Fix:** complete the check, name the Hebrew/Greek editions and English translations used, change *qara* to *qāraʿ* consistently, and replace the warning with a dated verification statement; keep unsighted material such as the Heraclides fragment out of load-bearing prose.

16. **[P2 should-fix] — C17 / Orion Correlation Theory** — A July 2026 JAHH article reports that the three-pyramid/Orion-Belt shape match is statistically unusual, although that does not establish ancient intent or rescue Bauval’s wider chronology and shaft program. The vault’s unqualified “proportions don’t match cleanly” should engage this new result rather than appear unaware of it ([2026 article](https://www.sciengine.com/JAHH/doi/10.3724/SP.J.1440-2807.2026.02.06)). **Fix:** audit its method and add it as recent evidence within C17’s still-contested status.

17. **[P2 should-fix] — C32, `Scripture Index.md`, `foundation/06 Heaven Leaking Through.md` / classical source chain** — Cicero makes the Milky Way the blessed souls’ dwelling or destination; Macrobius and Porphyry supply the two portals, while the cited Heraclides fragment supports a soul-path but not necessarily the gate doctrine. Saying Cicero calls the band itself a road conflates “a virtuous life is the road to heaven” with the following description of the Milky Way ([Cicero’s text](https://www.tertullian.org/fathers/cicero_dream_of_scipio_02_trans.htm)). **Fix:** attribute each component separately, verify Heraclides before use, call Macrobius influential rather than “the standard textbook,” and remove the unsupported inference that the one-sign error proves theological necessity.

18. **[P2 should-fix] — C30, Steelman support 5 / James versus Jacob** — The evidence securely concerns Saint James and regional *Camino/Jakobsweg* names; the patriarch Jacob and Ladder connection rests on one uncited Allen sentence and is explicitly contested. Yet it appears inside the supposedly load-bearing Steelman as “Jacob’s Road” drawn toward the patriarch. **Fix:** move support 5 to non-load-bearing exploration and say “Way of Saint James”; retain the patriarch/Ladder link only as a clearly labeled unresolved suggestion.

19. **[P2 should-fix] — O7, `foundation/02 The Designed Address.md`, Steelman support 4 / fine-tuning** — Cosmological fine-tuning of constants is not evidence for the separate local claim that this Galactic address is unusually habitable or measurable. The [SEP fine-tuning entry](https://plato.stanford.edu/archives/sum2026/entries/fine-tuning/) surveys the former debate, so “SEP’s judgment is ‘contested but not defeated’” needs an exact location or quotation marks removed; Weisberg’s alleged religious identity is irrelevant. **Fix:** separate the two arguments, identify *The Privileged Planet* specifically as an intelligent-design argument rather than generic “Christian astronomy,” and leave local-address consonance non-evidential.

20. **[P2 should-fix] — C3, `foundation/01 Our Galactic Address.md` / Galactic-center uncertainty** — GRAVITY 2019 reported 8178 ± 13 statistical ± 22 systematic pc, approximately 26,670 ± 80 ly, not ±1,000 ly ([GRAVITY result](https://www.aanda.org/articles/aa/full_html/2019/05/aa35656-19/aa35656-19.html)). **Fix:** give the cited uncertainty correctly or omit it and retain “about 26,700 ly.”

21. **[P2 should-fix] — C2 / Local Arm quotation** — “The spur interpretation is definitely incorrect” is wording from a later review, not a quotation from Xu et al. 2016 or Reid et al. 2019 themselves; the measurements establish an extended, star-forming arm-like segment but do not settle every global morphological label. **Fix:** attribute the quotation to [Shen and Zheng’s review](https://www.raa-journal.org/issues/all/2020/v20n10/speci/202203/P020220325569509655948.pdf), or paraphrase the measured findings without categorical quotation marks.

22. **[P2 should-fix] — O2 / “great circle”** — The formal Galactic equator is a great circle, while the observed luminous band only approximately follows it because of the Sun’s offset, disk warp, local structure, dust, and the bulge. “Every observer’s celestial sphere” is also wrong outside the disk midplane and unnecessary. **Fix:** say the band forms an approximately full-sky belt centered on the Galactic plane, as expected for an observer embedded in the disk.

23. **[P2 should-fix] — `foundation/01` and `foundation/03` / Orion nomenclature** — Constellations are projected patterns, not objects residing in an arm, and the Local/Orion Arm’s modern name supplies no physical or theological connection to the Orion asterism’s religious history. **Fix:** say most naked-eye stars are comparatively nearby but at different distances, and mark the shared Orion name as nomenclatural association or `#claim/pattern`, never support.

24. **[P2 should-fix] — O8 / status “answered”** — The divine-darkness material shows only that a dark or hostile location is not incompatible with selected theological traditions; it does not answer the arbitrariness of choosing the Galactic center or provide a reason to identify it as a thin place. **Fix:** change the status to “partially defused; no positive support,” and remove the EHT “pierced the veil” flourish.

25. **[P2 should-fix] — `foundation/05 Black Holes as Spiritual Paradigms.md` / Jude 13** — Jude 13 applies its “wandering stars” image to the condemned intruders or teachers introduced in Jude 4 and described in verses 12–13, not directly to fallen angels. **Fix:** write, “Jude likens the false teachers to wandering stars”; retain the black-hole mapping only as retired poetry.

26. **[P2 should-fix] — C23 and `foundation/05` / *tzimtzum*** — “God contracts His own light inward” compresses a complex Lurianic doctrine and blurs the distinction between Ein Sof and the withdrawal/concealment of *or Ein Sof*; later Jewish traditions also dispute literal versus nonliteral readings. **Fix:** cite Vital precisely, describe it as withdrawal or concealment of infinite light to make conceptual space, and state that the black-hole comparison is the project’s external analogy, not a Jewish teaching.

27. **[P2 should-fix] — `foundation/04 Space as Fabric.md` / physics framing** — “The structural mechanics of the heavens” and “worked material under tension” turn biblical poetic/cosmological imagery into mechanics, despite the audit’s anti-concordist posture. **Fix:** call these recurrent fabric metaphors and make every connection to expanding or dynamic spacetime explicitly interpretive.

28. **[P2 should-fix] — `explorations/Gematria Threads.md` / protocol compliance** — The rules require arithmetic to be shown, but the note gives only totals; 11 terms also produce 55 pairwise comparisons, and the classical 130 match is not a newly discovered hit from the same search. **Fix:** show every letter sum, identify the exact MT verse spelling and policy for prefixes/articles, document all 55 comparisons and candidate-selection timing, and remove “degrees of freedom low” unless quantitatively justified.

29. **[P2 should-fix] — `foundation/03` / “Modern mystics”** — The portal paragraph attributes a detailed doctrine to an undefined population with no practitioner source, failing the project’s rule that a tradition must demonstrably have taught what is reported. **Fix:** name and cite representative contemporary New Age or esoteric authors, date the teaching, and avoid generalizing them as “modern mystics.”

30. **[P2 should-fix] — `README.md` and foundation notes / “preserved intact”** — The foundation has plainly been corrected and annotated, so “the original hypothesis is preserved intact” is inaccurate; moreover, retired claims often remain as assertive lead sentences followed only later by a tag and walkback. **Fix:** say the original six-part structure is retained with audit annotations, preserve the untouched text through git history or an explicitly historical block, and put the current verdict before each retired claim so snippets cannot launder it.

31. **[P2 should-fix] — `stress-test/Steelman.md` / stale status section** — “Open work (updated after research round 1)” still lists raqia and Macrobius as Round 2 candidates even though the vault reports those as completed in rounds 3–4. **Fix:** update the section through round 4 and distinguish genuinely open work from completed dossiers.

32. **[P2 should-fix] — `The Hypothesis.md`, O6 / Version B rhetoric** — “This is coherent and cannot be ruled out” asserts coherence without supplying a model, while “Occam removes it” overstates what parsimony can do to a declared metaphysical commitment. **Fix:** say the vault treats B as a metaphysical possibility for which it supplies no observational test or astronomical evidence, and that it adds no empirical explanatory value.

33. **[P3 nit] — `Home.md` and internal links / GitHub presentation** — Obsidian `[[wikilinks]]` do not become ordinary clickable links in GitHub’s standard Markdown renderer, leaving the public-facing map difficult to navigate. **Fix:** add relative Markdown links to the public MOCs or generate a GitHub-compatible mirror while retaining wikilinks for Obsidian.

**Overall verdict: publishable after P1s.** The vault’s audit posture is promising, but the current public surface violates its own evidence rules, restores claims it explicitly found unattested, overstates the astronomy, and handles living cultural traditions too reductively. These are repairable through retagging, narrower wording, provenance, and clearer separation of documented inputs from the Christian theological inference; the exploratory and mystical material does not need to be removed.
