# Section 5 — Black Holes as Spiritual Paradigms

Black holes represent the breakdown of ordinary physical description, making them powerful metaphors for the supernatural. **Metaphors** — the audit is strict about that word here.

## The bottomless pit
The Bible references **Abyssos**, the bottomless pit. Black holes are regions where matter falls toward a central point without hitting a floor. `#claim/interpretation` — a legitimate and vivid metaphor; the Greek *abyssos* in its own context refers to the primeval deep / the underworld's depths, not an astronomical object.

## Wandering stars
Jude 13 likens **the false teachers** (introduced in Jude 4 and described in vv. 12–13 — referent precised, review round 1) to "wandering stars, for whom the blackness of darkness has been reserved forever." `#claim/misreading` when applied to black holes — *planētes asteres* ("wandering stars") was the standard ancient term for **the planets**, whose irregular paths made them emblems of instability. Jude's image is of lights that stray from their courses, not of collapsed stars trapping light. Retired as support; kept only as poetry. See [[Claims Audit]] C8.

## The divine retraction (tzimtzum)
In Lurianic Kabbalah (via Vital, *Etz Chaim*), the tzimtzum is the withdrawal or concealment of the infinite light (*or Ein Sof*) to make conceptual space in which creation can stand — noting the Ein Sof / *or Ein Sof* distinction and the live Jewish debate over literal vs. nonliteral readings (precision per review round 1). `#claim/solid` as a report of the doctrine. The black-hole comparison is **this project's external analogy, not a Jewish teaching** — `#claim/interpretation`, and a loose one: tzimtzum is withdrawal *to make room*, collapse is concentration *that closes room off*. The direction of the analogy is nearly opposite; keep it as a resonance, not a match.

## The memory bank (holographic principle)
Modern physics conjectures that information falling into a black hole is encoded on its 2D boundary. `#claim/contested` as physics — the holographic principle is a serious, influential conjecture (’t Hooft, Susskind, AdS/CFT), not an established fact about our universe, and the information-paradox debate is live. The mystical comparison (a cosmic record of all history) is `#claim/interpretation`.
