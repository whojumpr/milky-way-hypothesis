# Section 4 — Space as a Fabric (The "Rip" Metaphor)

The "rip in space" intuition resonates with the Bible's recurrent *fabric imagery* for the heavens — poetic/cosmological metaphor, not mechanics (framing per review round 1); every connection drawn to dynamic spacetime below is explicitly interpretive.

## The stretchable universe
The Bible repeatedly says God "stretches out the heavens like a curtain" or a tent (Isaiah 40:22; also Job 9:8, Psalm 104:2, Isaiah 42:5, 44:24, Jeremiah 10:12, Zechariah 12:1 — one of the most repeated cosmological images in scripture): the heavens as worked fabric. `#claim/solid` as a description of the recurring *metaphor*; `#claim/interpretation` whenever mapped onto expanding spacetime.

## The cosmic tear (rend)
In moments of divine entry, prophets pray for God to tear the heavens open — "Oh, that You would rend the heavens and come down" (Isaiah 64:1, Hebrew *qāraʿ*, to tear). At Jesus' baptism, Mark uses the Greek **schizō** — a violent rip — for the sky opening (Mark 1:10), where Matthew and Luke use "opened" (*anoigō*); reading that difference as Mark's deliberate intensification is redactional `#claim/interpretation`. The solid facts: Mark uses *schizō* exactly twice, at the baptism (1:10) and at the tearing of the temple veil (15:38), and Josephus (*Jewish War* 5.212–214, Loeb rendering) describes the temple's **outer** veil as portraying the heavens. The synthesis — "Mark's torn veil *is* a torn sky," a deliberate cosmic inclusio — is **Ulansey's published interpretation** (*JBL* 110/1, 1991), adopted here with its status labeled: Mark's veil-referent (inner vs. outer curtain) remains debated in scholarship. Still the strongest textual thread the hypothesis has — held at its correct rank in [[Steelman]] and C21.

## The scroll metaphor
At the end, the sky will "roll up like a scroll" (Revelation 6:14, Isaiah 34:4) — the heavens as a coherent sheet that can be rolled, split, and removed. `#claim/solid` as text; the claim that this "uncannily mirrors" general relativity's dynamic spacetime is `#claim/interpretation` — suggestive convergence, not prediction.
